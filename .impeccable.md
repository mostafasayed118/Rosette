## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are gift buyers choosing flowers or botanical gifts, frequently on mobile and often with limited time or incomplete certainty about the right arrangement. The review prioritizes browsing, comparing, customizing, and completing a purchase.

### Brand Personality
Calm, premium, and thoughtful. The experience should feel trustworthy, romantic, and artisanal rather than busy or overly promotional.

### Aesthetic Direction
Botanical editorial / minimalist-tactile: warm ivory canvas, deep rose conversion color, sage support color, Fraunces display typography, Outfit body typography, and restrained hairline borders. Preserve asymmetry and gallery-like imagery while keeping checkout practical and easy to scan. English, French, and Arabic should have equal visual quality, including RTL parity.

### Design Principles
1. Let the flowers and product photography lead; keep UI chrome quiet.
2. Make the purchase path obvious at every step, especially on small screens.
3. Use one semantic token system and reusable component states to prevent visual drift.
4. Treat localization, RTL, keyboard access, and reduced motion as first-class design constraints.
5. Prefer calm tonal transitions and clear hierarchy over decorative motion or excessive elevation.
